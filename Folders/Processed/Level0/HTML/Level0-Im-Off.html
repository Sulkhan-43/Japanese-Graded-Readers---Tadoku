Vocabulary

  • 木村きむら家け (Kimura-ke)the Kimura household
  • 毎日まいにち (mainichi)every day; daily life
  • いってきます (ittekimasu)a set phrase said when leaving: “I’m off.”
  • 朝あさ (asa)morning
  • 時じ (ji)o’clock; the counter used for clock time
  • 由美ゆみ (Yumi)Yumi, a family member
  • 言いう (iu)to say; to tell
  • おはよう (ohayō)good morning (casual greeting)
  • お母かあさん (okāsan)mother; Mom
  • 一郎いちろう (Ichirō)Ichiro, a family member

Grammar Notes

  • N の Nの links two nouns, showing a relationship such as possession, association, or description. In the title, it means the everyday life of the Kimura family.
  • N ですです makes a polite statement, often identifying or describing something.
  • N が Vが marks the subject performing an action; the story often uses this frame in its narration.
  • Vますます gives a verb a polite, non-past form, used here for actions and repeated narration.
  • N もも means “also” or “too,” adding another person or thing to what has already been mentioned.

Grammar Notes

  • Vましたました is the polite past form of a verb.
  • N と Vと can mark someone accompanying the subject; here it means “with a friend.”
  • N へ Vへ marks the direction or destination of movement; it is pronounced e.
  • N はは marks the topic—the person or thing the sentence is about.

Passage 9 of 9

木村きむらさんの家族かぞくは、お父とうさん、お母かあさん、一郎いちろう、由美ゆみの4人にんと、猫ねこのノン。

RomajiKimura-san no kazoku wa, otōsan, okāsan, Ichirō, Yumi no yonin to, neko no Non.

EnglishThe Kimura family is Father, Mother, Ichiro, Yumi, and their cat, Non.

今朝けさも、みんな元気げんきに出でかけます。

RomajiKesa mo, minna genki ni dekakemasu.

EnglishThis morning, too, everyone sets out in good spirits.

Vocabulary

  • 家族かぞく (kazoku)family
  • 今朝けさ (kesa)this morning
  • 人にん (nin)people counter; in 4人 it is read yonin, “four people”
  • みんな (minna)everyone
  • 元気げんき (genki)well; in good spirits
  • 出でかける (dekakeru)to go out; to set out

Grammar Notes

  • N と Nと connects nouns in a complete list, as in four people and a cat.
  • なA に VA な-adjective can take に to describe how an action is done.
