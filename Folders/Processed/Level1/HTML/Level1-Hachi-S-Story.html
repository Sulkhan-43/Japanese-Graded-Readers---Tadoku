Story summary

Hachi came to live at a university teacher's home. Hachi and the teacher were always together. One day, the teacher collapsed at the university... It is the story of the most famous dog in Japan.

“Hachi” wa, koko de daigaku no sensei ga, Hachi o moraimashita.

Here, a university teacher received Hachi.

Vocabulary
  • 大学だいがく (daigaku) — university
  • 先生せんせい (sensei) — teacher; professor
  • もらう (morau) — to receive; to be given

Vocabulary
  • 渋谷しぶや駅えき (Shibuya-eki) — Shibuya Station
  • 電車でんしゃ (densha) — train
  • 乗のる (noru) — to get on; to ride
  • 行いってきます (ittekimasu) — a phrase said when leaving home, implying one will return
  • 帰かえる (kaeru) — to return; to go home
Grammar Notes
  • Nへ行いく — Marks the destination of going; the particle is pronounced “e.”
  • Nで V — Marks the place where an action takes place.

The teacher does not return6 / 10
先生せんせいは、その日ひ、大学だいがくで倒たおれました。

Sensei wa, sono hi, daigaku de taoremashita.

That day, the teacher collapsed at the university.

そして、

Soshite,

And then,

大学だいがくから病院びょういんへ行いきました。

Daigaku kara byōin e ikimashita.

He went from the university to the hospital.

先生せんせいは、もう、帰かえりません。

Sensei wa, mō, kaerimasen.

The teacher will not return anymore.

先生せんせいは、もう、いません。

Sensei wa, mō, imasen.

The teacher is no longer here.

Vocabulary
  • 倒たおれる (taoreru) — to collapse; to fall down
  • 病院びょういん (byōin) — hospital
Grammar Notes
  • Vません — The polite negative form of a verb, used to say that someone does not do something.
  • もう + Vません — With a negative verb, this means that something no longer happens or someone is no longer present.
  • そして — Connects events in sequence, meaning “and then.”
