Kabo-chan's Autumn Trip to Japan

かぼちゃん、日本にほんの秋あきへ
5 study passagesJapanese · Romaji · English

Repeated vocabulary and grammar explanations are intentionally omitted after their first useful explanation.

Story summary

This is a story about Kabo-chan, a pumpkin character created by Japanese learner Ryu, traveling around Japan in autumn.

Arrival in Japan1 / 5
日本にほんにやって来きたかぼちゃんは、いろいろなところを旅たびします。

Nihon ni yatte kita Kabo-chan wa, iroiro na tokoro o tabishimasu.

Kabo-chan has come to Japan and is traveling to all kinds of places.

季節きせつは秋あきです。

Kisetsu wa aki desu.

It's autumn.

「楽たのしみだな」

Tanoshimi da na.

I can't wait.

Vocabulary
  • 日本にほん (Nihon) — Japan
  • 秋あき (aki) — autumn
  • 旅たび (tabi) — journey; trip
  • 季節きせつ (kisetsu) — season
  • 楽たのしみ (tanoshimi) — something to look forward to
  • いろいろ (iroiro) — various; all kinds of
  • ところ (tokoro) — place
  • やって来くる (yatte kuru) — to come; to arrive
Grammar Notes
  • Vた + N — A plain past verb before a noun modifies that noun, identifying which person or thing is meant.
  • な / なあ — At the end of an informal sentence, this adds a reflective or impressed tone.

Sweet Potatoes and Sweets3 / 5
焼やき芋いも

Yakiimo

Roasted sweet potato

おいしそう！！

Oishisō!!

That looks delicious!!

町まちには、かぼちゃのお菓子かしが並ならんでいます。

Machi ni wa, kabocha no okashi ga narande imasu.

Pumpkin sweets are lined up around town.

Vocabulary
  • 焼やき芋いも (yakiimo) — roasted sweet potato
  • おいしい (oishii) — delicious
  • 町まち (machi) — town
  • かぼちゃ (kabocha) — pumpkin
  • お菓子かし (okashi) — sweets; confectionery
  • 並ならぶ (narabu) — to line up; to be lined up
Grammar Notes
  • 〜そう — Attach this to an adjective stem to say that something looks or seems that way.
  • Nには — This marks a place as the setting or topic, often meaning 'in' or 'as for' that place.
  • 〜ている — This form can describe an ongoing action or a resulting state; here it describes sweets being lined up.
Halloween4 / 5
ん？

N?

Hm?

あれは何なにだろう？

Are wa nan darō?

What's that?

ハロウィンのイベントです。

Harowin no ibento desu.

It's a Halloween event.

通とおりは、ハロウィンの服ふくを着きた人ひとでいっぱいです。

Tōri wa, Harowin no fuku o kita hito de ippai desu.

The street is full of people wearing Halloween clothes.

「みんな、かわいいなあ」

Minna, kawaii nā.

Everyone is so cute.

Vocabulary
  • 何なに (nani) — what
  • ハロウィン (Harowin) — Halloween
  • イベント (ibento) — event
  • 通とおり (tōri) — street
  • 服ふく (fuku) — clothes
  • 着きる (kiru) — to wear; to put on
  • 人ひと (hito) — person; people
  • いっぱい (ippai) — full; a lot
  • みんな (minna) — everyone
  • かわいい (kawaii) — cute
Grammar Notes
  • Nでいっぱい — This means a place is full of the people or things named before it.
Shibuya5 / 5
ハロウィンの日ひは、渋谷しぶやの町まちは人ひとでいっぱいです。

Harowin no hi wa, Shibuya no machi wa hito de ippai desu.

On Halloween, the streets of Shibuya are full of people.

次つぎは、どこに行いこうかな。

Tsugi wa, doko ni ikō kana.

Where shall I go next?

かぼちゃんの旅たびは続つづきます。

Kabo-chan no tabi wa tsuzukimasu.

Kabo-chan's journey continues.

Vocabulary
  • 日ひ (hi) — day
  • 渋谷しぶや (Shibuya) — Shibuya
  • 次つぎ (tsugi) — next
  • 行いく (iku) — to go
  • 続つづく (tsuzuku) — to continue
Grammar Notes
  • V-volitional + かな — This expresses a tentative self-directed thought, such as wondering what to do next.
