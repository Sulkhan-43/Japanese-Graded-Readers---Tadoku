This Is a Mexican Dog1 / 9
これはメキシコの犬いぬです。

Kore wa Mekishiko no inu desu.

This is a dog from Mexico.

Vocabulary
  • メキシコ (Mekishiko) — Mexico
  • 犬いぬ (inu) — dog
Grammar Notes
  • N wa — Wa marks the topic of a sentence, showing what the sentence is about.
  • N no N — No links two nouns; the first noun describes or is related to the second.
  • N desu — Desu makes a polite statement or identifies something; it can also follow an adjective.
The Xoloitzcuintle2 / 9
ショロイツクインツレ（ショロ）です。

Shoroitsukuintsure (Shoro) desu.

It is a Xoloitzcuintle (Xolo).

Vocabulary
  • ショロ (Shoro) — Xolo; short name for the Xoloitzcuintle
  • ショロイツクインツレ (Shoroitsukuintsure) — Xoloitzcuintle, a Mexican dog breed
Small and Large Xolos3 / 9
小ちいさいショロと大おおきいショロがいます。

Chiisai Shoro to ookii Shoro ga imasu.

There are small Xolos and large Xolos.

Vocabulary
  • 小ちいさい (chiisai) — small
  • 大おおきい (ookii) — large
  • いる (iru) — to exist; used for people and animals
Grammar Notes
  • N ga imasu — Imasu states that a person or animal exists; ga marks the person or animal being described.
Smooth and Hot4 / 9
ショロは毛けがありません。

Shoro wa ke ga arimasen.

Xolos have no hair.

つるつるです。

Tsurutsuru desu.

They are smooth.

ハアハア。とても暑あついです。

Hāhā. Totemo atsui desu.

Pant, pant. It is very hot.

Vocabulary
  • 毛け (ke) — hair; fur
  • ある (aru) — to exist; to have
  • つるつる (tsurutsuru) — smooth; sleek
  • ハアハア (Hāhā) — panting sound
  • とても (totemo) — very
  • 暑あつい (atsui) — hot (weather)
Grammar Notes
  • N ga arimasu / arimasen — Arimasu and arimasen express that a thing exists or does not exist; they can also describe having something.
Xolo Colors5 / 9
黒くろや茶色ちゃいろや灰色はいいろのショロがいます。そして、白しろいぶちのショロもいます。

Kuro ya chairo ya haiiro no Shoro ga imasu. Soshite, shiroi buchi no Shoro mo imasu.

There are black, brown, and gray Xolos. There are also Xolos with white spots.

Vocabulary
  • 黒くろ (kuro) — black
  • 茶色ちゃいろ (chairo) — brown
  • 灰色はいいろ (haiiro) — gray
  • 白しろい (shiroi) — white
  • ぶち (buchi) — spot; patch of color
Grammar Notes
  • N ya N ya N — Ya lists examples without implying that the list is complete.
  • N mo — Mo means also or too, and replaces particles such as ga in this sentence.
Dogs of the Aztec Era6 / 9
ショロはアステカ時代じだい（1325年ねんから1521年ねん）の犬いぬです。

Shoro wa Asuteka jidai (1325-nen kara 1521-nen) no inu desu.

Xolos are dogs of the Aztec era (from 1325 to 1521).

Vocabulary
  • アステカ (Asuteka) — Aztec
  • 時代じだい (jidai) — era; period
Grammar Notes
  • N kara — Kara marks a starting point and means from.
Xolos in Aztec Legends7 / 9
アステカの伝説でんせつではショロは神かみの使つかいです。死しんだ人ひとを死者ししゃの世界せかいに案内あんないします。

Asuteka no densetsu de wa Shoro wa kami no tsukai desu. Shinda hito o shisha no sekai ni annai shimasu.

In Aztec legends, Xolos are messengers of the gods. They guide the dead to the world of the dead.

Vocabulary
  • 伝説でんせつ (densetsu) — legend
  • 神かみ (kami) — god
  • 使つかい (tsukai) — messenger; servant
  • 死しぬ (shinu) — to die
  • 人ひと (hito) — person
  • 死者ししゃ (shisha) — the dead; deceased people
  • 世界せかい (sekai) — world
  • 案内あんない (annai) — guidance; showing the way
Grammar Notes
  • N de wa — De wa sets a place or context as the setting for the statement, meaning in or as for that context.
  • V-ta + N — A verb in the ta-form can come before a noun to describe someone or something that did the action.
  • N ni annai suru — Ni marks the destination that someone is guided to with annai suru.
Spotted Dogs8 / 9
案内あんないした犬いぬには、ぶちがあります。

Annai shita inu ni wa, buchi ga arimasu.

The dogs that guided them have spots.

Grammar Notes
  • N ni wa — Ni wa marks the person or thing in relation to which something is true; here it means for the dogs.
Special and Important Dogs9 / 9
だから、ショロはメキシコでは、特別とくべつでとても大切たいせつな犬いぬです。

Dakara, Shoro wa Mekishiko de wa, tokubetsu de totemo taisetsu na inu desu.

So Xolos are very special and very important dogs in Mexico.

Vocabulary
  • 特別とくべつ (tokubetsu) — special
  • 大切たいせつ (taisetsu) — important; precious
Grammar Notes
  • na-adj + de — De connects na-adjectives, with the sense of and.
  • na-adj + na + N — A na-adjective uses na before a noun to describe it.
