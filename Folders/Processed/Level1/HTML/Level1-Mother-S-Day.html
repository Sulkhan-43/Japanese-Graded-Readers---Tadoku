The Date1 / 15
5月がつの第だい2日曜日にちようびは、母ははの日ひです。

Gogatsu no dai ni nichiyōbi wa, haha no hi desu.

The second Sunday in May is Mother's Day.

今日きょうは母ははの日ひだ!

Kyō wa haha no hi da!

Today is Mother's Day!

Vocabulary
  • 5月がつ (gogatsu) — May
  • 日曜日にちようび (nichiyōbi) — Sunday
  • 母ははの日ひ (haha no hi) — Mother's Day
  • 今日きょう (kyō) — today
Grammar Notes
  • N no N — Links two nouns, often showing a relationship or specifying what kind of thing the second noun is.
  • dai + number — The prefix dai before a number marks an ordinal, such as a numbered Sunday in the month.
  • N wa — Marks the topic of a sentence: what the speaker is talking about.
  • N desu — A polite way to state or identify something using a noun.
Getting Ready2 / 15
お母かあさんにプレゼントを買かいたい。

Okaasan ni purezento o kaitai.

I want to buy a present for Mom.

お父とうさんもいっしょに行いこうか?

Otōsan mo issho ni ikō ka?

Dad, shall we go together?

ううん、二人ふたりで行いきたい!

Uun, futari de ikitai!

No, I want the two of us to go!

Vocabulary
  • お母かあさん (okaasan) — mother; mom
  • プレゼント (purezento) — present; gift
  • 買かう (kau) — to buy
  • お父とうさん (otōsan) — father; dad
  • いっしょ (issho) — together
  • 行いく (iku) — to go
  • 二人ふたり (futari) — two people; the two of us
  • ううん (uun) — no (casual)
Grammar Notes
  • N ni — Marks a recipient, destination, or the setting of an action, depending on the phrase.
  • N mo — Adds the meaning 'also' or 'too' to a noun or noun phrase.
  • V-tai — Attaches to a verb stem to say that the speaker wants to do something.
  • V-volitional — The volitional form is used to suggest doing something together, like 'let's go.'
  • ka (question) — Placed at the end of a sentence to form a question or suggestion.
  • N de — Marks how or with whom an action is done; here it means 'as a pair' or 'the two of us.'

Jā, kore.

Then, here.

Vocabulary
  • うん (un) — yeah (casual)
  • だいじょうぶ (daijōbu) — all right; okay
  • じゃあ (jā) — then; well then

Choosing Flowers6 / 15
赤あかいカーネーション、ください。

Akai kānēshon, kudasai.

Red carnations, please.

はい。赤あかですね。

Hai. Aka desu ne.

Yes. Red, right?

あっ、ピンクも!

Ah, pinku mo!

Oh, pink too!

えっ、ピンクも?

Eh, pinku mo?

Huh, pink too?

うん、お母かあさん、ピンクも好すきだよ!

Un, okaasan, pinku mo suki da yo!

Yeah, Mom likes pink too!

Vocabulary
  • 赤あかい (akai) — red
  • 赤あか (aka) — red; the color red
  • ください (kudasai) — please (when making a request)
  • はい (hai) — yes; certainly
  • ピンク (pinku) — pink
  • 好すき (suki) — to like
Grammar Notes
  • N (object) + kudasai — Adds a polite 'please' to a requested item; the object particle is often omitted in these examples.
  • ne — A sentence ending that invites agreement or adds a gentle, confirming tone.

  • どうぞ (dōzo) — here you are; please
Grammar Notes
  • to — Connects nouns with the meaning 'and.'
