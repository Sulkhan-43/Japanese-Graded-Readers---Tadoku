Meeting Yumi1 / 15
ぼくは、くまのぬいぐるみ。

Boku wa, kuma no nuigurumi.

I'm a stuffed bear.

2000年ねん、ゆみちゃんのうちに来きました。

2000-nen, Yumi-chan no uchi ni kimashita.

In 2000, I came to Yumi-chan's home.

ゆみちゃん

Yumi-chan.

Yumi-chan.

Vocabulary
  • ぼく (boku) — I; me, typically used by boys and men
  • 家か族ぞく (kazoku) — family
  • くま (kuma) — bear; teddy bear
  • ぬいぐるみ (nuigurumi) — stuffed toy; plush toy
  • 年ねん (nen) — year; used after a number to state a year
  • うち (uchi) — home; one's house
  • 来くる (kuru) — to come
Grammar Notes
  • N wa — Wa marks the topic of a sentence, often translated as “as for” or left implicit in English.
  • N no N — No links two nouns to show possession or association, as in a person's home or a family's member.
  • V-masu / V-mashita — Masu forms make verbs polite; masu is nonpast, while mashita marks the past.

Yoku waraimasu.

She laughs a lot.

よくうんちします。

Yoku unchi shimasu.

She poops a lot.

Vocabulary
  • よく (yoku) — often; a lot
  • 食たべる (taberu) — to eat
  • 泣なく (naku) — to cry
  • 笑わらう (warau) — to laugh; to smile
  • うんちする (unchi suru) — to poop
Yumi loves me3 / 15
ゆみちゃんは、ぼくが大だい好すきです。

Yumi-chan wa, boku ga daisuki desu.

Yumi-chan loves me very much.

ときどき、ぼくを食たべます・・・。

Tokidoki, boku o tabemasu...

Sometimes, she eats me...

Vocabulary
  • 大だい好すき (daisuki) — to love; to like very much
  • ときどき (tokidoki) — sometimes
Grammar Notes
  • N ga suki / daisuki — Use ga with suki or daisuki to mark the person or thing someone likes.
Always together4 / 15
ぼくとゆみちゃんは、ず～っといっしょにいました。

Boku to Yumi-chan wa, zū tto issho ni imashita.

Yumi-chan and I were together the whole time.

ぼくは、ゆみちゃんの一番いちばんのかぞく。

Boku wa, Yumi-chan no ichiban no kazoku.

I'm Yumi-chan's number one family member.

Vocabulary
  • ずっと (zutto) — all along; for a long time; always
  • 一緒いっしょ (issho) — together
  • いる (iru) — to be; to exist (for people and animals)
  • 一番いちばん (ichiban) — number one; best
Grammar Notes
  • N to — To links nouns with the meaning “and” or “with,” depending on the sentence.
A white dress5 / 15

2023-nen.

2023.

白しろいドレスのゆみちゃん。

Shiroi doresu no Yumi-chan.

Yumi-chan in a white dress.

きれいね～。

Kirei ne~.

So pretty.

Vocabulary
  • 白しろい (shiroi) — white
  • ドレス (doresu) — dress
  • きれい (kirei) — pretty; beautiful
Grammar Notes
  • i-adjective + N — An i-adjective can come directly before a noun to describe it.
  • sentence-final ne — Ne at the end of a sentence invites agreement or adds a warm, conversational tone.
