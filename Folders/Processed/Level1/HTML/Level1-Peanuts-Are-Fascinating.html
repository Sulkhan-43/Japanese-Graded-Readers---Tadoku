What is a peanut?1 / 17
これは落花生らっかせいです。

Kore wa rakkasei desu.

This is a peanut.

落おちる？

Ochiru?

Fall?

花はな？

Hana?

Flower?

生うまれる？

Umareru?

Be born?

どうして、落花生らっかせいという名前なまえなんでしょう。

Dōshite, rakkasei to iu namae nan deshō.

Why do you think it is called a peanut?

Vocabulary
  • 落花生らっかせい (rakkasei) — peanut; peanut plant
  • おもしろい (omoshiroi) — interesting; fascinating
  • 落おちる (ochiru) — fall
  • 花はな (hana) — flower
  • 生うまれる (umareru) — be born; come into being
  • 名前なまえ (namae) — name
  • いう (iu) — say; call
Grammar Notes
  • N to iu N — Use this pattern to identify or name something as a particular noun, as in “a peanut called by this name.”

Why the name?17 / 17
花はなが落おちて、生うまれるから落花生らっかせいという名前なまえです。

Hana ga ochite, umareru kara rakkasei to iu namae desu.

It is called a peanut because the flower falls and a peanut is born.

① 花はなが咲さく

Ichi: hana ga saku.

1. The flower blooms.

② 花はなが枯かれて、子房柄しぼうへいが出でてくる

Ni: hana ga karete, shibōhei ga dete kuru.

2. The flower withers, and the ovary stalk emerges.

③ 子房柄しぼうへいが土つちの中なかに入はいって、実みができる

San: shibōhei ga tsuchi no naka ni haitte, mi ga dekiru.

3. The ovary stalk enters the soil, and fruit forms.

おもしろいですね！

Omoshiroi desu ne!

Isn't that interesting!

Grammar Notes
  • clause kara — Kara after a clause gives the reason for what follows: “because ...”.
  • V-te kuru — Kuru after a te-form can describe something emerging or moving toward the speaker's point of view.
