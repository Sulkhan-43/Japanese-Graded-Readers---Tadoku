JAPANESE GRADED READER · LEVEL 1

The Bus

バス
11 study passagesJapanese · Romaji · English

Repeated vocabulary and grammar explanations are intentionally omitted after their first useful explanation.

Story summary

Masao Suzuki is a bus driver. One night, a passenger gets on his bus. It is Mr. Suzuki's grandmother, who died ten years earlier.

The Driver1 / 11
鈴木すずき正雄まさおさんは、二十歳はたちです。

Suzuki Masao-san wa, hatachi desu.

Masao Suzuki is twenty years old.

バスの運転手うんてんしゅです。

Basu no untenshu desu.

He is a bus driver.

毎日まいにち、「上田うえだ」から「丸山まるやま」まで、

Mainichi, “Ueda” kara “Maruyama” made,

Every day, from Ueda to Maruyama,

バスを運転うんてんします。

Basu o unten shimasu.

he drives a bus.

Vocabulary
  • 二十歳はたち (hatachi) — twenty years old
  • 運転手うんてんしゅ (untenshu) — driver
  • 毎日まいにち (mainichi) — every day
  • バス (basu) — bus
Grammar Notes
  • 〜から〜まで — Marks a starting point and an ending point: “from ... to ...”.

Grammar Notes

The Truck Ahead3 / 11

Mae, ōkii torakku ga imasu.

Hayaku netai nā.

Just then, the big truck in front stopped.

「うわあ！！危あぶない！」

“Uwā!! Abunai!”

“Whoa! Look out!”

Vocabulary
  • 仕事しごと (shigoto) — work; job
  • 帰かえる (kaeru) — to return; to go home
  • 寝ねる (neru) — to sleep; to go to bed
  • 危あぶない (abunai) — dangerous; look out
  • 後うしろ (ushiro) — behind; the back
Grammar Notes
  • 〜たい — Attach this to a verb stem to say that you want to do something.
  • 〜とき — Means “when” or “at the time”; it can refer to a particular moment.
