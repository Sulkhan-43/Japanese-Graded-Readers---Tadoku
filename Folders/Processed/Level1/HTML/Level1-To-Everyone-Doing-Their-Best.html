To Everyone Doing Their Best

Story page 11 / 8
みなさんのお仕事しごとは忙いそがしいですか。

Minasan no oshigoto wa isogashii desu ka?

Are you busy with your work?

毎日まいにち疲つかれませんか。

Mainichi tsukaremasen ka?

Don't you get tired every day?

Vocabulary
  • 頑張がんばる (ganbaru) — to do one's best
  • みなさん (minasan) — everyone; you all
  • 仕事しごと (shigoto) — work; job
  • 忙いそがしい (isogashii) — busy
  • 毎日まいにち (mainichi) — every day
  • 疲つかれる (tsukareru) — to get tired; tiredness
Grammar Notes
  • V-te iru — Shows an ongoing action or continuing state; here, someone is in the state of doing their best.
  • N no N — Links two nouns, often showing possession or association.
  • V-masen ka — A polite negative question used here to gently ask about someone's condition.
Story page 22 / 8
毎日まいにち、私わたしは学校がっこうもアルバイトも忙いそがしいです。

Mainichi, watashi wa gakkō mo arubaito mo isogashii desu.

Every day, I'm busy with both school and my part-time job.

とても疲つかれました。

Totemo tsukaremashita.

I got very tired.

Vocabulary
  • 学校がっこう (gakkō) — school
  • アルバイト (arubaito) — part-time job
  • とても (totemo) — very
Grammar Notes
  • mo — Means “also” or “too,” and can mark each item in a pair to include both.

星ほしもたくさん見みえます。

Hoshi mo takusan miemasu.

I can see lots of stars, too.

Vocabulary

  • たくさん (takusan) — many; a lot
  • 見みえる (mieru) — to be visible; to be able to see
Story page 77 / 8
きれいな星ほしと月つき見みると、疲つかれも寂さびしさもだんだんなくなります！

Kirei na hoshi to tsuki o miru to, tsukare mo sabishisa mo dandan nakunarimasu!

When I look at the beautiful stars and the moon, my tiredness and loneliness gradually disappear!

Vocabulary
  • だんだん (dandan) — gradually; little by little
  • 見みる (miru) — to look at; to see
Grammar Notes
  • na-adjective + na + N — A na-adjective takes na when it comes directly before a noun.
  • N to N — Connects two nouns with the meaning “and.”
  • V-ru to — After a plain verb, to can mean “when” and introduce a result that follows.
  • I-adjective-ku naru — Changes an i-adjective into its ku form before naru to describe a change of state.
Story page 88 / 8
疲つかれたとき、みなさんも空そらを見上みあげてみましょうね。

Tsukareta toki, minasan mo sora o miagete mimashō ne.

When you're tired, let's try looking up at the sky, too.

Vocabulary
  • 空そら (sora) — sky
  • 見上みあげる (miageru) — to look up at
Grammar Notes
  • V-ta toki — Uses the past form before toki to mean “when” or “at the time of” doing something.
  • V-te mimashou — A polite suggestion to try doing something.
  • ne — A sentence-ending particle that softens a suggestion and invites agreement.
