Urashima Taro

浦島うらしま太郎たろう
11 study passagesJapanese · Romaji · English

Repeated vocabulary and grammar explanations are intentionally omitted after their first useful explanation.

Story summary

One day, Taro saves a turtle. In gratitude, the turtle takes him to the Dragon Palace beneath the sea. Taro spends a happy time there, but... This is a very famous folktale.

The seaside1 / 11
「浦島うらしま太郎たろう」は、日本にほんの古ふるい話はなしです。

“Urashima Tarō” wa, Nihon no furui hanashi desu.

“Urashima Taro” is an old Japanese story.

ここは、海うみの近ちかくです。

Koko wa, umi no chikaku desu.

This is near the sea.

太郎たろうとお母かあさんのうちがあります。

Tarō to okāsan no uchi ga arimasu.

Taro and his mother have a home.

太郎たろうは、毎日まいにち、海うみへ行いきます。

Tarō wa, mainichi, umi e ikimasu.

Taro goes to the sea every day.

そして、魚さかなをとります。

Soshite, sakana o torimasu.

And he catches fish.

Vocabulary
  • 海うみ (umi) — sea
  • 魚さかな (sakana) — fish
  • 毎日まいにち (mainichi) — every day
  • 近ちかく (chikaku) — nearby; vicinity
Grammar Notes
  • N へ — This pattern marks the direction or destination of movement.
The turtle2 / 11
今日きょうも、太郎たろうは海うみへ行いきました。

Kyō mo, Tarō wa umi e ikimashita.

Today, Taro went to the sea again.

海うみに、子こどもがたくさんいます。

Umi ni, kodomo ga takusan imasu.

There are many children by the sea.

子こどもたちが、棒ぼうで何なにかをたたきました。

Kodomotachi ga, bō de nanika o tatakimashita.

The children were hitting something with a stick.

亀かめです。

Kame desu.

It is a turtle.

子こどもたちは、棒ぼうで亀かめをたたきます。

Kodomotachi wa, bō de kame o tatakimasu.

The children hit the turtle with a stick.

「痛いたい！痛いたい！」亀かめは泣なきました。

“Itai! Itai!” kame wa nakimashita.

“Ouch! Ouch!” the turtle cried.

太郎たろうは、子こどもたちに言いいました。

Tarō wa, kodomotachi ni iimashita.

Taro said to the children,

「私わたしに、この亀かめをください」

“Watashi ni, kono kame o kudasai.”

“Please give me this turtle.”

「本当ほんとう？いいよ」

“Hontō? Ii yo.”

“Really? All right.”

子こどもたちは、亀かめを太郎たろうにあげました。

Kodomotachi wa, kame o Tarō ni agemashita.

The children gave the turtle to Taro.

Vocabulary
  • 亀かめ (kame) — turtle
  • たたく (tataku) — to hit; to strike
  • 痛いたい (itai) — painful; ouch
  • たくさん (takusan) — many; a lot
Grammar Notes
  • N をください — This pattern is used to politely ask for an item.

The Dragon Palace5 / 11
亀かめと太郎たろうは、大おおきい城しろの前まえに来きました。

Kame to Tarō wa, ōkii shiro no mae ni kimashita.

The turtle and Taro came to a great castle.

「ここは竜宮城りゅうぐうじょうですよ」亀かめが言いいました。

“Koko wa Ryūgūjō desu yo,” kame ga iimashita.

“This is the Dragon Palace,” said the turtle.

竜宮城りゅうぐうじょうはとてもきれいです。

Ryūgūjō wa totemo kirei desu.

The Dragon Palace is very beautiful.

太郎たろうは、亀かめと一緒いっしょに竜宮城りゅうぐうじょうの中なかへ入はいりました。

Tarō wa, kame to issho ni Ryūgūjō no naka e hairimashita.

Taro went into the Dragon Palace with the turtle.

そこには、とてもきれいな女おんなの人ひとがいました。

Soko ni wa, totemo kirei na onna no hito ga imashita.

There was a very beautiful woman there.

太郎たろうは、亀かめに聞ききました。

Tarō wa, kame ni kikimashita.

Taro asked the turtle,

「あのきれいな女おんなの人ひとは、誰だれですか」

“Ano kirei na onna no hito wa, dare desu ka?”

“Who is that beautiful woman?”

「乙姫おとひめさまですよ」

“Otōhime-sama desu yo.”

“That is Princess Otohime.”

亀かめは答こたえました。

Kame wa kotaemashita.

The turtle answered.

「あなたが太郎たろうさんですね。

“Anata ga Tarō-san desu ne.

“You must be Taro.

さあ、こちらへどうぞ」乙姫おとひめさまは太郎たろうに言いいました。

Sā, kochira e dōzo,” Otōhime-sama wa Tarō ni iimashita.

“Please come this way,” Princess Otohime said to Taro.

Vocabulary
  • 竜宮城りゅうぐうじょう (Ryūgūjō) — Dragon Palace under the sea
  • 乙姫おとひめさま (Otōhime-sama) — Princess Otohime; the princess of the Dragon Palace
A happy stay6 / 11

Oishii osake mo takusan nomimashita.

Every day is very enjoyable.

Isshūkan, nishūkan…, ikkagetsu, nikagetsu…, ichinen, ninen….

Taro wants to go home7 / 11

Aru hi, Otōhime-sama ga iimashita.

“Sō desu ka.

The song and ending11 / 11
むかしむかし 浦島うらしまは

Mukashi mukashi Urashima wa

Long, long ago, there was Urashima.

助たすけた亀かめに連つれられて

Tasuketa kame ni tsurerarete

He was taken along by the turtle he had saved.

竜宮城りゅうぐうじょうへ来きてみれば

Ryūgūjō e kite mireba

When he arrived at the Dragon Palace,

絵えにもかけない美うつくしさ

E ni mo kakenai utsukushisa

It was too beautiful to paint.

乙姫おとひめ様さまのごちそうに

Otōhime-sama no gochisō ni

At Princess Otohime’s feast,

鯛たいや比目魚ひらめの舞踊まいおどり

Tai ya hirame no mai-odori

The sea bream and flounder danced.

ただ珍めずらしく面白おもしろく

Tada mezurashiku omoshiroku

Everything was strange and delightful.

月日つきひの経たつも夢ゆめのうち

Tsukihi no tatsu mo yume no uchi

The passing days and months seemed like a dream.

遊あそびにあきて気きがついて

Asobi ni akite ki ga tsuite

He grew tired of playing and came to his senses.

お暇乞いとまごいもそこそこに

O-itomagoi mo soko soko ni

After barely saying goodbye,

帰かえる途中とちゅうの楽たのしみは

Kaeru tochū no tanoshimi wa

The pleasure on his way home was

土産みやげにもらった玉手箱たまてばこ

Miyage ni moratta tamatebako

The jewel box he had received as a souvenir.

帰かえってみればこは如何いかに

Kaette mireba ko wa ikani

When he returned, what did he find?

元もと居いた家いえも村むらもなく

Moto ita ie mo mura mo naku

The house and village where he once lived were gone.

路みちに行ゆきあう人々ひとびとは

Michi ni yukiau hitobito wa

The people he met on the road

顔かおも知しらない者ものばかり

Kao mo shiranai mono bakari

Were all strangers to him.

心細こころぼそさに蓋ふたとれば

Kokorobososa ni futa toreba

Feeling uneasy, when he lifted the lid,

あけて悔くやしき玉手箱たまてばこ

Akete kuyashiki tamatebako

The jewel box was one he would regret opening.

中なかからばっと白煙しろけむり

Naka kara batto shirokemuri

A burst of white smoke came from inside.

たちまち太郎たろうはお爺じいさん

Tachimachi Tarō wa ojīsan

In an instant, Taro became an old man.

それから、太郎たろうはどこへ行いきましたか。

Sore kara, Tarō wa doko e ikimashita ka.

Where did Taro go after that?

それは、だれもわかりません。

Sore wa, dare mo wakarimasen.

No one knows.
