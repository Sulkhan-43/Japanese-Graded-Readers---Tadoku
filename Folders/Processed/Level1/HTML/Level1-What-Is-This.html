Story page 11 / 17
これは何なんでしょう？

Kore wa nan deshō?

What could this be?

豚ぶたの形かたちですね。

Buta no katachi desu ne.

It is shaped like a pig, isn't it?

夏なつに使つかいます。

Natsu ni tsukaimasu.

You use it in summer.

Vocabulary
  • 何なん (nan) — what
  • 使つかう (tsukau) — to use
Grammar Notes
  • ~ deshō — Adds a tentative or wondering tone to a question, like “what could it be?”

  • 煙けむり (kemuri) — smoke
  • 死しぬ (shinu) — to die

Story page 55 / 17
これは何なんでしょう？

Kore wa nan deshō?

What could this be?

いろいろな色いろや模様もようがあります。

Iroiro na iro ya moyō ga arimasu.

There are many colors and patterns.

日本にほんのどの家いえにもあります。

Nihon no dono ie ni mo arimasu.

There is one in every Japanese home.

Vocabulary
  • 模様もよう (moyō) — pattern
Grammar Notes
  • dono N ni mo — With dono, ni mo means “in every” or “at any,” as in every home.
  • N ya N — Ya lists examples, often meaning “N and other things like N.”

Story page 1212 / 17
「だるま」です。

Daruma desu.

It's a daruma.

目めを一ひとつ描かきます。そして、お願ねがいします。

Me o hitotsu kakimasu. Soshite, onegaishimasu.

Draw one eye. Then make a wish.

「大学だいがくに合格ごうかくしますように」

Daigaku ni gōkaku shimasu yō ni.

May I be accepted into university.

もう一ひとつの目めを描かきます。

Mō hitotsu no me o kakimasu.

Draw the other eye.

「だるまさん、ありがとう!」

Daruma-san, arigatō!

Daruma, thank you!

Vocabulary
  • だるま (daruma) — daruma doll
  • 描かく (kaku) — to draw
  • 合格ごうかくする (gōkaku suru) — to pass; to be accepted
Grammar Notes
  • V-masu yō ni — Used in a wish or prayer, meaning “may ...” or “I hope ...”.
