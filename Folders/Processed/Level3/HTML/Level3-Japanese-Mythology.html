One: The Beginning of Japan

Sekai wa hajime, ten mo umi mo chi mo hakkiri shite imasen deshita.

At first, heaven, the sea, and the earth were not clearly defined.

しかし、あるとき、天てんと地ちができて、たくさんの神様かみさまが生うまれました。

But one day, heaven and earth came into being, and many gods were born.

ある日ひ、高天原たかまがはら（天てんの国くに）に住すんでいる神様かみさまたちが会議かいぎをしました。

Aru hi, Takamagahara (ten no kuni) ni sunde iru kamisamatachi ga kaigi o shimashita.

One day, the gods living in Takamagahara, the heavenly realm, held a meeting.

そして、若わかい男女だんじょの神様かみさまを呼よびました。

Soshite, wakai danjo no kamisama o yobimashita.

Then they summoned a young god and a young goddess.

男おとこの神様かみさまは「伊邪那岐いざなぎ」、女おんなの神様かみさまは「伊邪那美いざなみ」といいます。

Otoko no kamisama wa Izanagi, onna no kamisama wa Izanami to iimasu.

The god was called Izanagi, and the goddess was called Izanami.

神様かみさまたちは、二人ふたりに、長ながい長ながい矛ほこを渡わたして言いいました。

「二人ふたりで一緒いっしょに国くにを作つくりなさい」

Futari de issho ni kuni o tsukurinasai.

伊邪那岐いざなぎは、伊邪那美いざなみを探さがして暗くらい道みちをどんどん歩あるいていきました。

Izanagi wa, Izanami o sagashite kurai michi o dondon aruite ikimashita.

Grammar Notes
  • てしまう — This pattern marks a completed action or result, often with regret or an unintended outcome.
  • ながら — Attached to a verb stem, this pattern means doing one action while doing another.
  • てくれる — This pattern presents someone else's action as a favor to the speaker or someone close to the speaker.
  • ことができる — After a dictionary-form verb, this pattern expresses ability or possibility. The topic particle can replace the subject particle for emphasis.
  • てみる — This pattern means trying an action to see what happens.
  • ないでください — This pattern politely asks someone not to do something.
  • いくら〜ても — This pattern means no matter how much or how long an action is done, the stated result remains unchanged.
  • なくなる — Following a negative verb form, this pattern describes becoming unable to do something or no longer doing it.
  • てはいけない — This pattern expresses a prohibition: an action must not be done.
  • のに — This pattern means although or despite, often highlighting an unexpected or disappointing result.
