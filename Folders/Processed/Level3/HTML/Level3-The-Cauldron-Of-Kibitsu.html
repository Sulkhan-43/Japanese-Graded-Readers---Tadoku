Story summary

After abandoning his wife Isora, Shotaro begins living with Sode. But their happy life together does not last long. A very frightening tale from Tales of Moonlight and Rain.

Oyu ga waita toki, gō to oto ga dereba, sono kekkon ya shigoto wa shite ii ga, oto ga denakereba, shite wa ikenai.

When the water boils, if it makes a roaring sound, they may go ahead with the marriage or work; if it makes no sound, they must not.
