The Crane’s Return of a Favor

Story summary

One day, a beautiful crane comes down in the rice fields where Yosaku is working. Kind Yosaku helps the injured crane.

Opening and Yosaku’s life1 / 14
鶴つるは、白しろくて美うつくしい鳥とりです。

Tsuru wa, shirokute utsukushii tori desu.

The crane is a white and beautiful bird.
