The Shadow Lover

Story summary

After an argument, Takayuki leaves. The one who speaks to Yuko, left behind in the room, is Takayuki's shadow...!?

Grammar Notes
  • たことはなかった — This pattern says that an action had never happened before a past reference point. The topic marker adds contrast.
  • ないでください — This pattern attaches to a verb's negative form to politely ask someone not to do something.
  • お〜になる — This pattern respectfully describes someone else's action using a verb stem between the prefix and ending.
  • ようだ — This pattern expresses an impression or inference: something seems or appears to be a certain way.
