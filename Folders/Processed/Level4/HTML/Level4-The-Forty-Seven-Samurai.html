Story summary

In 1701, Lord Asano attacked Kira in Edo Castle and was ordered to commit seppuku. His retainers heard the news and spent nearly two years preparing to avenge him. On December 14, 1702, forty-seven of them attacked Kira's residence and killed him. They later committed seppuku themselves. This true story of revenge is also known as Chūshingura.

Akō no rōnintachi wa, tōtō Kira o utsu koto ga dekita.

The ronin of Ako had finally succeeded in killing Kira.

大石おおいしは言いった。

Ōishi wa itta.

Oishi spoke.

「みんな、長ながい間あいだご苦労くろうであった。

'Minna, nagai aida gokurō de atta.

'Everyone, thank you for your long efforts.

これから泉岳寺せんがくじに行いって、殿とのに吉良上野介きらこうずけのすけを討うったことを知しらせよう」

Kore kara Sengakuji ni itte, tono ni Kira Kōzuke no Suke o utta koto o shiraseyō.'

Now let us go to Sengakuji and tell our lord that we have killed Kira Kozuke no Suke.'

九きゅう 泉岳寺せんがくじへ

Kyū: Sengakuji e

Nine: To Sengakuji

浅野家あさのけの墓はかは泉岳寺せんがくじにある。

Asano-ke no haka wa Sengakuji ni aru.

The Asano family's graves were at Sengakuji.

赤穂あこうの浪人ろうにんたちは吉良きらの首くびを槍やりにつけて泉岳寺せんがくじへ向むかった。

Akō no rōnintachi wa Kira no kubi o yari ni tsukete Sengakuji e mukatta.

The ronin of Ako put Kira's head on a spear and headed for Sengakuji.

朝あさになって多おおくの人々ひとびとが家いえから出でてきた。

Asa ni natte ōku no hitobito ga ie kara dete kita.

Morning came, and many people emerged from their houses.

「赤穂あこうの浪人ろうにんたちが吉良きらを殺ころしたそうだ」

'Akō no rōnintachi ga Kira o koroshita sō da.'

'I hear that the ronin of Ako have killed Kira.'

「二年近にねんちかくも準備じゅんびして殿様とのさまの敵討かたきうちをした。

'Ninen chikaku mo junbi shite tonosama no katakiuchi o shita.

'They prepared for nearly two years and avenged their lord.

この人ひとたちこそ本当ほんとうの侍さむらいだ」

Kono hitotachi koso hontō no samurai da.'

These men are true samurai.'

この敵討かたきうちのことは、その日ひのうちに江戸中えどじゅうに伝つたわった。

Kono katakiuchi no koto wa, sono hi no uchi ni Edojū ni tsutawatta.

News of this revenge spread throughout Edo that very day.

赤穂あこうの浪人ろうにんたちは泉岳寺せんがくじに着つくと、吉良きらの首くびを浅野内匠頭あさのたくみのかみの墓はかに置おいた。

Akō no rōnintachi wa Sengakuji ni tsuku to, Kira no kubi o Asano Takumi no Kami no haka ni oita.

When the ronin of Ako arrived at Sengakuji, they placed Kira's head at Asano Takumi no Kami's grave.

大石おおいしは言いった。

Ōishi wa itta.

Oishi spoke.

「殿との、お待またせしました。

'Tono, omatase shimashita.

'My lord, we have kept you waiting.

やっと、殿とのの敵かたき、吉良上野介きらこうずけのすけを討うちました。

Yatto, tono no kataki, Kira Kōzuke no Suke o uchimashita.

At last, we have killed your enemy, Kira Kozuke no Suke.

私わたしたちは、今いまも殿とのの家来けらいです」

Watashitachi wa, ima mo tono no kerai desu.'

We are still your retainers.'

赤穂あこうの浪人ろうにんたちはいつまでも墓はかの前まえを動うごかなかった。

Akō no rōnintachi wa itsu made mo haka no mae o ugokanakatta.

The ronin of Ako remained before the grave for a long time without moving.

一七〇三年二月四日せんななひゃくさんねんにがつよっか、大石おおいしをはじめ赤穂あこうの浪人ろうにんたちは切腹せっぷくした。

Sen nanahyaku sannen nigatsu yokka, Ōishi o hajime Akō no rōnintachi wa seppuku shita.

On February 4, 1703, Oishi and the other ronin of Ako committed ritual suicide.

墓はかは浅野内匠頭あさのたくみのかみと同おなじ

Haka wa Asano Takumi no Kami to onaji

Their graves were in the same... as Asano Takumi no Kami's [the supplied transcript ends mid-sentence].
