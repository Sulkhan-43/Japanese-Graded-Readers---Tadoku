Kyonen no jūnigatsu, koko Yanaginoshita-machi ni chiisana tokoya "Yū" o hiraita.

Last December, I opened a small barber shop called Yu here in Yanaginoshita.

柳之下町やなぎのしたまちは古ふるい町まちで、瑞桜寺ずいおうじという大おおきなお寺てらがある。

Yanaginoshita-machi wa furui machi de, Zuiōji to iu ōkina otera ga aru.

Yanaginoshita is an old town with a large temple called Zuioji.

「優ゆう」はそのお寺てらのお墓はかの裏うらにある。

"Yū" wa sono otera no ohaka no ura ni aru.

Yu is behind that temple's cemetery.

はっきり言いって場所ばしょは良よくない。

Hakkiri itte basho wa yokunai.

To be frank, the location is not good.

建物たてものも古ふるいし、お墓はかと店みせの間あいだに大おおきな桜さくらの木きがあって、昼間ひるまでも日ひがあまり当あたらない。

Tatemono mo furui shi, ohaka to mise no aida ni ōkina sakura no ki ga atte, hiruma demo hi ga amari ataranai.

The building is old, and there is a large cherry tree between the cemetery and the shop, so it gets little sunlight even during the day.

だが、家賃やちんは安やすい。

Daga, yachin wa yasui.

But the rent is cheap.

それに、柳之下町やなぎのしたまちは、三さん、四年前よねんまえから新あたらしいマンションや団地だんちが建たって住人じゅうにんが増ふえている。

Sore ni, Yanaginoshita-machi wa, san, yo-nen mae kara atarashii manshon ya danchi ga tatte jūnin ga fuete iru.

Besides, new apartment buildings and housing estates have been built in Yanaginoshita over the last three or four years, and the population is growing.

お金かねはないが、自分じぶんの店みせがほしい。

Okane wa nai ga, jibun no mise ga hoshii.

I have no money, but I want my own shop.

ということで、悩なやんで悩なやんでここに決きめた。

To iu koto de, nayande nayande koko ni kimeta.

So, after agonizing over it for a long time, I decided on this place.

最初さいしょはお客きゃくさんがぜんぜん来こなかった。

Saisho wa okyakusan ga zenzen konakatta.

At first, no customers came at all.

でも、がんばって近所きんじょにチラシを配くばったら、だんだんお客きゃくさんが来くるようになった。

Demo, ganbatte kinjo ni chirashi o kubattara, dandan okyakusan ga kuru yō ni natta.

But when I worked hard to distribute flyers around the neighborhood, customers gradually began to come.

それでも、平日へいじつはなかなかお客きゃくさんが増ふえないので、先月せんげつから新あたらしいサービスを始はじめた。

Sore demo, heijitsu wa nakanaka okyakusan ga fuenai node, sengetsu kara atarashii sābisu o hajimeta.

Even so, weekday customer numbers were slow to grow, so last month I started offering some new services.

一ひとつは、夜よる7時じまでだった営業時間えいぎょうじかんを9時じまでに延のばすことだ。

Hitotsu wa, yoru shichiji made datta eigyō jikan o kuji made ni nobasu koto da.

One was to extend my opening hours from seven in the evening to nine.

平日へいじつ、会社員かいしゃいんが仕事帰しごとがえりでも来きやすいようにした。

Heijitsu, kaishain ga shigoto-gaeri demo kiyasui yō ni shita.

I made it easier for office workers to come on their way home from work on weekdays.

もう一ひとつのサービスは、子こども割引わりびきだ。

Mō hitotsu no sābisu wa, kodomo waribiki da.

The other service is a children's discount.

カットのみでほかの店みせでは1000円えんぐらいだが、うちの店みせは思おもい切きって777円えんにした。

Katto nomi de hoka no mise de wa sen-en gurai da ga, uchi no mise wa omoikitte nanahyaku nanajū nana-en ni shita.

Other shops charge about a thousand yen for just a haircut, but I took the plunge and set my shop's price at 777 yen.

子こどもを連つれて来くるお父とうさんやお母かあさんも、お客きゃくさんになってくれると思おもったからだ。

Kodomo o tsurete kuru otōsan ya okāsan mo, okyakusan ni natte kureru to omotta kara da.

That was because I thought the fathers and mothers who brought their children would become customers too.

Grammar Notes
  • NというN — This pattern introduces the name of something, meaning a person or thing called a particular name.
  • し — This pattern lists reasons or related facts, often implying that there are others. A sentence may end with it and leave the conclusion understood.
  • たら — This pattern can mean when or after an action occurred, followed by the result that actually happened.
  • ようになる — This pattern describes a change in behavior or circumstances, such as beginning to do something regularly.
  • ます-stemやすい — This pattern attaches to a verb stem to express that an action is easy to do.
  • ようにする — This pattern expresses taking steps or arranging things so that a desired situation becomes possible.
  • Nにする — This pattern expresses choosing or setting something, such as a price or an age limit.
  • てくれる — This pattern presents another person's action as helpful or welcome from the speaker's perspective.

  • より — This pattern marks the standard of comparison. Here it compares the woman's actual height with what the narrator had expected.
