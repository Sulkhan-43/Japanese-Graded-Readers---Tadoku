Vocabulary
  • イルミネーション (iruminēshon) — decorative lighting
  • 過すごす (sugosu) — to spend time
Grammar Notes
  • Adjective stem + そう — This pattern describes an impression based on appearance: someone or something looks a certain way.
  • Verb stem + ながら — This pattern means doing one action while doing another. The same person performs both actions.

Vocabulary
  • 旅人たびびと (tabibito) — traveler
  • 人気にんき (ninki) — popularity; being popular
  • 早速さっそく (sassoku) — right away; without delay
  • いらっしゃいませ (irasshaimase) — welcome, said to a customer
  • かしこまりました (kashikomarimashita) — certainly; a formal acknowledgment of a request
Grammar Notes
  • にも～にも — This pattern repeats the same marking to include both groups equally. Here the cafe is popular with travelers and island residents alike.
